Design Scenario

For assignment 1, I have chosen scenario 5 (Dynamic Soundtracker), which asks for an instrument that generates an ongoing and evolving soundtrack that relates to a specific activity. The user may contribute through small and infrequent inputs, but these actions remain meaningful. From my interpretation of this scenario, I intend for its purpose to allow users to build a soundtrack passively while they are simultaneously completing the activity they're already doing, as opposed to an active and continuous performance from the user. The worth of this is in making an often unseen process, such as time spent studying, or focusing visible through the means of an evolving soundscape and UI elements. This grants users a display that illustrates the time spent, which is something that often remains invisible.

Design Response

My response to this scenario is a curated branching, almost vine structure that continues to grow across the screen over the course of a study session or lengthy activity, with each vine's growth spaced over time, using the technique of Math.random() to determine the length, direction, and sound of each new stem. This allows for a new branch to be added to the structure as well as a new layer of sound to the ambient soundtrack. The overall result is unique to that specific study session this creates varying outcomes defined by how long the user chooses to focus on the activity.

My curated growing garden is a browser-based ambient instrument that generates an evolving soundtrack for focused activities such as studying or being creative. Rather than requiring the user to actively perform inputs, this browser-based instrument builds its soundtrack passively over the course of a session. As time passes, a branching structure of thin and thick stems grow across the screen, with each new branch adding a layer of audio to the soundscape. The Math.random() extended technique controls both the structure's visual growth, branch angle, length and placement. Addtionally it will support the audio's evolution through layering and timing aspects, merging the visual and audio together as a single generative system.

Responding to Purpose

Scenario 5 explores creating an ongoing soundtrack suited to a chosen activity, where the user's input may be minimal or infrequent but still meaningful. My branch growing instrument responds to this by making the duration of time spent focusing on an activity itself the key input. This decision to make the input passive means the user doesn't need to continuously make interactions to generate feedback from the browser or to curate their soundtrack. Simply having the page open while they study or work is enough to build an evolving piece of music. I still intend to include an optional means of a light interaction, an example would be using a click to manually trigger the growth of a vine or branch, but the instrument's system is designed to function meaningfully without continuous interaction needed.

Responding to Worth

This chosen scenario's context emphasises that the time spent on quiet activities like studying can feel intangible, the user can be in deep focus, meaning hours pass often with little to show for them beyond the work completed itself. My browser-based instrument's worth lies in making that course of time audible and visible. A study session's length and character can become represented in a unique, unrepeatable display of growth and sound. No two sessions will generate the same result, so the output becomes a kind of memento of that specific time of focus. This transforms the invisible time spent into a visible and tangible presence, to something that would otherwise remain unmarked. In choosing plant growth particulury vines and branches, is symbolic of easily overlooked moments as these plants spread persistently and complexly over time demonstarting how a seemingly uneventful focus session can still be something intricate.

Design Framing

My browser based instrument will be framed through techniques that optimise randomness and conceptually experimental means. Technically, it will be built using standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, rendering its branching growth from the HTML <canvas> element and generating audio through the Web Audio API or a wrapper library such as Tone.js. I intend to source my audio layers ethically, ensuring to credit attributed sources. I will use Math.random() as my extended technique to govern both the branch's visual growth and the audio's evolution. No other extended technique is used to keep the generative style singular. Conceptually, my project draws on ideas of passive generativity and ambient music player systems that produce music as background noise rather than through direct performance. It excludes features that require the user's active, continuous input, so no keyboard/mouse playing this browser will also remove multiplayer or social features in order to create a focused environment for the user's focus session. The style and characterisation of my webpage is subtle and considered due to its intention of being passive and intended for focus. I want to avoid it becoming playful this means excluding overwhelming patterns and colour choices that feature heavy saturated shades. Instead, I want to opt for a calmer approach using desaturated, muted tones like silvers, greens and greys. In terms of background, a darker, more moody style may be fitting to create a non-distracting page. The branch stems themselves will feel sperratic and almost illustrative in apperence using a dark black to mimic the appearance of ink could be effective here. As my project's theme is around plants and growth i will follow existing visual concepts of vines and branches however i don't want it to be too realistic as this may take away from the intention of it being an abstract measure of a specific session of study.
